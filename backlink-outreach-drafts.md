## Quora — BMI for Indians

**Target question:** "What is a healthy BMI for Indians?" / "Is BMI 24 overweight?"

Depends which scale you're using — this trips a lot of people up.

The **global WHO scale** says:
- Normal: 18.5–24.9
- Overweight: 25–29.9
- Obese: 30+

But India uses a **stricter, India-specific scale** (2009 Indian Consensus Statement on adult obesity):
- Normal: 18.5–22.9
- Overweight: 23–24.9
- Obese: 25+

The reason isn't arbitrary — at the same BMI, South Asians carry more body fat, particularly more visceral (abdominal) fat, than white European populations, and develop type 2 diabetes and cardiovascular risk at lower BMI levels. WHO recognised this for Asian populations generally back in 2004; Indian physicians tightened the cutoffs further in 2009 based on Indian-specific data.

So if a generic calculator told you BMI 24 is "normal" — under the scale actually used in Indian clinical practice, that's overweight.

## Quora — protein intake / how much protein should I eat

**Target question:** "How much protein do I need per day?" / "How much protein per kg for muscle gain?"

There isn't one universal number — it depends on how active you are, and most "eat X grams" answers online skip that entirely.

- **Sedentary adult (baseline reference intake):** ~0.8 g/kg/day
- **Active adult, doing aerobic or resistance training:** 1.2–2.0 g/kg/day (American College of Sports Medicine)
- **Resistance training specifically:** upper half of that range
- **Broader active-population guidance:** 1.4–2.0 g/kg/day (International Society of Sports Nutrition)

So for a 70 kg person lifting weights regularly, that's realistically 84g–140g/day depending on training load — not a single fixed number.

## Quora — how much exercise per week

**Target question:** "How many minutes of exercise should I do per week?"

Per WHO's 2020 guidelines (the current standard most national health bodies reference):

- **150–300 minutes/week** of moderate-intensity aerobic activity, OR
- **75–150 minutes/week** of vigorous-intensity activity, OR an equivalent mix of both
- **Plus** muscle-strengthening exercise for all major muscle groups on **2+ days a week**

Going beyond 300 min moderate (or 150 min vigorous) gives additional benefit, but the ranges above are the actual floor, not a suggestion — a lot of "30 minutes a day" advice you see quoted is a rough simplification of this.
