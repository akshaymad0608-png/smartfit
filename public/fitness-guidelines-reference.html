Fitness & Nutrition Guidelines Reference

The actual recommendations behind FitSmart's calculators — not rounded-off advice, the real numbers from WHO, ACSM and the Indian Consensus Statement, each with its source so you can check it yourself.

List last checked: 14 September 2026 · By FitSmart · Always verify against the primary source for medical or clinical decisions.

Why this page exists

Most fitness sites quote guideline numbers without saying where they came from, or quietly round them into one figure. This page keeps the source attached to every number, and states outright when a range exists instead of collapsing it to a single value.

Protein guidance is genuinely given as a range, not one number — a 70 kg active adult's target is realistically anywhere from 84g to 140g/day depending on training load. FitSmart's macro calculator uses this range, not a single fixed multiplier.

Frequently asked questions

How many minutes of exercise per week does WHO recommend?
150-300 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic activity per week, or 75-150 minutes of vigorous-intensity activity, plus muscle-strengthening exercise for all major muscle groups on 2 or more days a week (WHO, 2020 guidelines).

How much protein should an active adult eat per kg of body weight?
1.2-2.0 g/kg/day, with the higher end for resistance training, per ACSM and the International Society of Sports Nutrition. A sedentary adult's baseline reference intake is around 0.8 g/kg/day.

What BMI is considered obese in India?
25 and above, per the 2009 Indian Consensus Statement — lower than the global WHO cutoff of 30, because Indians carry more visceral fat at the same BMI.
